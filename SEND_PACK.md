# Send pack

Everything needed to run outreach. Nothing here goes out without Shimon's go-ahead.

## Before the first message

**The Bit number is missing.** Nobody can pay until Shimon supplies it. Do not invent one.

**WhatsApp cannot start new chats** until roughly 7 August (restriction imposed 31 July).
So for every prospect the first move is Shimon's, from his phone:

1. Save the number as a contact on the phone.
2. Send one word from the phone: `היי`
3. Tell Claude the chat is open, and Claude runs the rest from the desktop app.

Without step 2 there is no conversation to have.

## The sequence

Never the link in message one. Never the price before he asks. Two lines a message.

| # | When | Message |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | opener | `היי, שמעון.` / `שאלה אחת ואני עף: כמה פעמים ביום אתה עוצר באמצע תספורת בשביל לענות לטלפון?` |

| 5 | he says no | `סבבה, תודה ובהצלחה.` and stop |

## Looking a prospect up, one at a time

B144 hides most numbers behind an 076 proxy, so the number comes from Google Maps. Do this per
prospect at the moment of contact, never in bulk — bulk searching got the session throttled.

Search the shop name plus its city on Google Maps and read off:

- **the phone.** 05x is a mobile and can take WhatsApp. 02, 03, 04, 08, 09 is a landline and cannot
- **the website.** No site, or only a b144 or israelbusinessguide listing, means he is a prospect.
  A real site means a different conversation
- **rating and review count.** Good ratings with no website is the sweet spot
- Then check B144 too. אבי אביב published a mobile there that Maps never showed
