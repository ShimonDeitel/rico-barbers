## The walkable cluster

הלל 5, אחד העם 12, ג'ורג' וושינגטון 2 and יואל משה סלומון 4 are all within a few minutes of each
other in the centre. יפו 217 is a short walk further. Five shops in one trip, and walking in beats
any message: he sees the shop, the barber sees a person, and nothing can flag an account.

## When to go

Mid-morning or mid-afternoon on Sunday through Thursday. Not Friday, they close early. Not the
minute they open and not right before closing. If the barber is mid-haircut, that is the pitch
demonstrating itself, so wait and let him finish.

## What to say

The opener is a question, not a pitch: how many times a day do you stop mid-haircut to answer the
phone. Then: that is what I fix, the customer books himself. The link only if he asks to see it.
The price only if he asks, 100 a month, in Bit. On a no, thank him and leave. Full script and
objection handling in `SELLING.md`.
